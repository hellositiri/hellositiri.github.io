Where your document goes, and where the names stay

  1. On your computer

    Open a Word file, PDF or scan in Sitiri. Tick the names it finds and add any it missed. Save the redacted copy and your key.

  2. Out for review

    Send the copy to a colleague, an outside reviewer or an AI tool. They see placeholders like [Company 1], never the names.

  3. Back with you

    Load the key and the reviewed copy. Every name returns, with comments and tracked changes where the reviewer left them.

Your documents stay on your computer

  • No uploads. Sitiri works inside your browser. There is no server behind it that could receive a file.
  • Works offline. Open it once and it keeps working with the internet switched off.
  • You keep the key. The list that links placeholders to names is saved only where you put it.

Check it yourself in a minute

Open Sitiri, switch off Wi-Fi, and redact a document: it still works. Or press F12, open the Network tab, and watch. Nothing is sent while it works.
