The work travels. The names stay with you.

Sitiri swaps each name for a placeholder such as [Company 1] or [Person 2], so the person reviewing the document never sees who it is about. Your key is what turns the placeholders back into names.

  1. On your computer

    Open a Word file, a PDF or a scan. Sitiri finds names, addresses and numbers; you tick them and add any it missed. Save the covered copy and your key.

  2. Out for review

    Send the copy to a colleague, an outside reviewer or an AI tool. They work on placeholders, never on your client's details.

  3. Back with you

    Load your key and the reviewed copy. Every name returns, with comments and tracked changes exactly where the reviewer left them.

Your documents never leave your computer.

There is no server behind Sitiri that could receive a file. It is a page that does its work inside your browser, and it keeps working with the internet switched off.

  • No uploads

    Documents are read and changed on your own machine.

  • Works offline

    Open it once and it runs without the internet.

  • You keep the key

    The list linking placeholders to names is saved only where you put it.

Check it yourself in a minute

Open Sitiri and switch off Wi-Fi: it still works. Or press F12, open the Network tab and watch. Nothing is sent.
